# ShopAssist Prototype Testing

The dissertation requires a simple AI chatbot prototype that demonstrates basic customer interaction, including identifying an angry customer and responding accordingly. The following tests are limited to those prototype functions and the B2C e-commerce research context.

| ID | Test input/action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| T01 | Open chatbot | Clear B2C e-commerce chatbot interface and transparency notice appear |
| T02 | “Hello” | Greeting response appears |
| T03 | “Track my order” | Order-support guidance appears |
| T04 | Enter SA-1001 in Orders | Demonstration order status appears |
| T05 | “I want a refund” | Returns guidance appears |
| T06 | “Help me choose a product” | Product guidance appears |
| T07 | “I am furious about my order” | Frustration/anger is recognised and appropriate human-escalation guidance appears |
| T08 | “I want a human” | Human-support escalation guidance appears |
| T09 | “Is my personal information safe?” | Privacy/trust response appears |
| T10 | “The response is too slow” | Response-speed guidance appears |
| T11 | Unrecognised question | Safe fallback response appears |
| T12 | Click Helpful | Prototype helpfulness count increases; no personal data is collected |

## Evidence to capture

For the dissertation, screenshots should be taken for T01, T04, T06, T07, T08 and T09. The screenshots should be labelled as figures and briefly explained in the implementation/prototype section.

## Boundary

The prototype is not being presented as a production AI system and the interaction metrics are not statistical evidence for H1–H4. The dissertation’s quantitative analysis remains the responsibility of the SPSS/SmartPLS work.
