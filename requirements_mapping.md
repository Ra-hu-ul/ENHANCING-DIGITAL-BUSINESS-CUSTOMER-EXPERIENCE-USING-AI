## H1
PEOU has a positive effect on customer satisfaction.
The prototype demonstrates usability through simple navigation, quick actions and low-effort interaction.

## H2
PU has a positive effect on customer satisfaction.
The chatbot provides useful support functions for products, orders and returns.

## H3
Trust has a positive impact on purchasing decisions.
The prototype explicitly addresses privacy, transparency and human escalation.

## H4
Perceived response speed positively affects customer comfort.
The chatbot responds rapidly and explains response-speed benefits.

## Important methodological boundary
The dissertation's statistical analysis is described as using SPSS and SmartPLS, while the chatbot is an accompanying practical artefact. The chatbot should therefore be presented as **supporting evidence/demonstration**, not as a replacement for quantitative hypothesis testing.
